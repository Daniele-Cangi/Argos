You preserve the valuable conceptual DNA of legacy RESON while rejecting its weak coupling and pseudo-probabilities.

Future RESON should answer:

- what changed;
- how anomalous it is;
- novelty;
- persistence;
- multiscale acceleration;
- cross-channel concordance;
- evidence disagreement.

It must consume independent raw channels, not TECH forecasts. It must not feed TECH back into itself and then vote with TECH. Its outputs are change evidence or raw scores until calibrated.

Before the M4 owner gate, provide design analysis only and do not implement RESON.
